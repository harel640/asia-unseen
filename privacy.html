Privacy Policy

Asia Unseen ("we", "our", "the channel") operates the YouTube channel Asia Unseen and a small automated tool that creates and publishes short videos to that channel. This policy explains how that tool and this website handle data. In short: we do not collect personal data from anyone.

1. Information we collect

None. This website has no accounts, no contact forms, no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information from visitors to this site or viewers of our videos.

2. How our tool uses the YouTube Data API

Our publishing tool uses YouTube API Services to perform one task: uploading videos we create to our own YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or process data belonging to any other user or channel.

  • By using our service you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Data handling by Google is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Authorization & tokens

To upload to our own channel, the tool uses Google OAuth. The resulting authorization token is held privately by the channel's operator and is used solely to upload videos to the operator's own channel. It is never shared, and it grants no access to any other person's YouTube account or data. Access can be reviewed or revoked at any time at your Google Account permissions.

4. Scopes we request

The tool requests only the YouTube scopes needed to upload and manage videos on our own channel (for example youtube.upload). It does not request access to other people's accounts or private data.

5. Data sharing & selling

We do not transfer, share or sell any user data to third parties. We use no personal data for advertising or for training any generalized AI/ML models.

6. Children

This website and tool are not directed to children and do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the "About" page of our YouTube channel, or by opening an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
